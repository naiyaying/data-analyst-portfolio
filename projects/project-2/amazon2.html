Project Overview

Businesses generate massive amounts of transaction data every day. Using SQL, this project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights by answering real-world analytical questions.

The analysis covers sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, geographic distribution, and operational metrics using aggregation, filtering, grouping, ranking, and time-based analysis.

Featured SQL Analysis

Below are several business questions answered using SQL. Each analysis includes the business question, SQL query, query result, and a brief business insight.

1. Total Revenue

Business Question

How much total revenue was generated from all transactions?

SQL Query 

SELECT
    SUM(TotalAmount) AS Total_Revenue
FROM amazon;

Result

$91.83M

Business Insight

The company generated more than $91.83 million in revenue, providing a strong foundation for evaluating overall business performance.

2. Total Transactions

Business Question

How many transactions occurred during the analysis period?

SQL Query 

SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS Total_Transactions
FROM amazon;

Result

100,000

Business Insight

The dataset contains 100,000 transactions, making it large enough to identify meaningful business patterns and trends.

3. Average Order Value

Business Question

What is the average transaction value?

SQL Query 

SELECT
    AVG(TotalAmount) AS Average_Order_Value
FROM amazon;

Result

$918.26

Business Insight

On average, each customer transaction generated approximately $918.26, indicating relatively high purchase values.

4. Highest Revenue Category

Business Question

Which product category generated the highest revenue?

SQL Query 

SELECT
    Category,
    SUM(TotalAmount) AS Revenue
FROM amazon
GROUP BY Category
ORDER BY Revenue DESC;

Result

Electronics

Business Insight

Electronics generated the highest revenue, making it the company's strongest performing product category.

Additional SQL Analysis

The following analyses provide additional business insights obtained from the dataset using SQL.

5. Top Selling Product

Business Question

Which product generated the highest revenue?

SQL Query 

SELECT
    ProductName,
    SUM(TotalAmount) AS Revenue
FROM amazon
GROUP BY ProductName
ORDER BY Revenue DESC
LIMIT 10;

Result

LED Desk Lamp

Business Insight

LED Desk Lamp generated the highest revenue, indicating consistently strong customer demand.

6. Top Customer

Business Question

Who spent the most across all transactions?

SQL Query 

SELECT
    CustomerName,
    SUM(TotalAmount) AS TotalSpent
FROM amazon
GROUP BY CustomerName
ORDER BY TotalSpent DESC
LIMIT 10;

Result

Pooja Kapoor

Business Insight

The highest-spending customer contributed significantly more revenue than the average customer, highlighting the importance of customer retention.
